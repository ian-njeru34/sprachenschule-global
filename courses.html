German language courses

German A1 to B2

The online course prices below reflect the prices currently published on the existing website. Physical classes are listed as KES 5,000 additional per level; confirm current availability and total fees with the school before payment.

LevelStageCourse focusPublished online fee
German A1BeginnerEssential vocabulary, grammar and everyday communication.KES 10,000
German A2ElementaryPractical communication and stronger sentence structures.KES 15,000
German B1IntermediateMore independent communication for everyday and work situations.KES 25,000
German B2Upper intermediateComprehension, speaking and professional communication.KES 30,000
