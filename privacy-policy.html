Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sprachenschule (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit sprachenschule.co.ke, enquire about courses, use an account, participate in lessons or communicate with us.

Privacy contact: sprachenschuleglobal@gmail.com

1. Information we may collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address and telephone number supplied when enquiring, registering or being enrolled.
  • Account and learning information: account identifier, role (student, tutor or registrar), course, language level, cohort, class assignment, enrolment or approval status and lesson participation information.
  • Content: documents, audio, video, messages and other materials users choose to upload or share.
  • Classroom information: display name, room and connection information, and audio, camera or screen-sharing streams while those features are in use.
  • Technical information: device/browser details, log data and IP address where made available by the services we use.
  • Enquiry and transaction information: details supplied when asking about courses or arranging a purchase. Payment credentials may be handled by the relevant payment provider.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries; create and administer accounts; manage enrolment, approvals, courses, cohorts and classes; deliver lessons and learning materials; operate classroom and communication features; administer purchases where applicable; maintain security; troubleshoot; comply with law; and protect users and the school.

3. Online classes, streams and YouTube archives

When you join an online classroom, your microphone, camera or shared screen may be transmitted to authorised participants according to the classroom controls. Check what your devices are transmitting before sharing.

If a class is scheduled for a YouTube live stream or archive, the class may be transmitted to the school's connected YouTube channel and an archive may be retained by YouTube. Access settings and private-viewer permissions are managed through YouTube. A private link alone may not grant access; a viewer may need to sign in with an authorised Google account. The school should notify participants before a class is streamed or archived. Contact the school before class if you have concerns about appearing in a stream.

YouTube features are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. You can review or revoke third-party access at Google Account permissions.

4. Service providers and sharing

We may use providers for website hosting, account and file storage, online classroom connectivity, communications, payments and video services. Depending on the feature, providers may include Vercel, Supabase, LiveKit, Google and YouTube, and payment or messaging providers selected by the school. Information is shared with providers as needed to operate the relevant service and with authorised school personnel to administer learning. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users or the school. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

5. International processing

Some providers may process or store information outside Kenya. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to use providers and arrangements intended to protect information under applicable data-protection requirements.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for education administration, account operation, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by record and provider. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your credentials confidential and notify us of suspected unauthorised access.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, ask us to restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for service, recordkeeping or legal reasons. We may verify your identity before responding. Contact sprachenschuleglobal@gmail.com.

8. Children and learner accounts

Where a learner is a child, registration and use should be arranged by a parent, guardian or the school as appropriate. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning a learner's information. We do not knowingly invite children to provide unnecessary personal information.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or its providers may use essential cookies, local storage or similar technologies for sign-in, security and basic operation. Third-party features may use their own technologies. Disabling essential technologies may affect functionality.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services, providers or legal obligations change. The current version and update date will appear on this page. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email sprachenschuleglobal@gmail.com.
