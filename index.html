Certification

Examining Bodies We Prepare You For

Our courses align with internationally recognised examination bodies, so your certificate is valid wherever your journey takes you.

German

Goethe-Institut

The most widely recognised German language certification worldwide, offering exams from A1 to C2. Accepted by employers, universities, and embassies globally.

German

telc

Europe's leading provider of language certificates, with exams aligned to the CEFR framework. Recognised for work, study, and immigration purposes in Germany.

German

ÖSD

The Austrian German Language Diploma, offering internationally recognised exams from A1 to C2. Accepted by universities and employers in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland.

German

TestDaF & DSH

University-level German exams required for admission to German universities. We offer preparation support for both at B2 level and above.

Spanish

DELE & SIELE

Official Spanish proficiency exams issued by the Instituto Cervantes. Recognised internationally for study, work, and migration.

French

DELF & DALF

Official French diplomas issued by France Éducation International. DELF covers A1–B2, while DALF covers C1–C2.

English

IELTS, TOEFL & Cambridge

Globally recognised English proficiency exams for university admission, migration, and professional registration.

Swahili

KNEC & CBET

Swahili proficiency assessed through Kenyan national examination frameworks and competency-based education and training.
