Career Fields

Where our scholars work

Explore high-demand career pathways for Kenyan professionals and international applicants in Germany.

High Demand

🏥 Nursing (Pflegefachkraft)

Germany faces a critical shortage of nurses. Kenyan nurses with B1/B2 German are in strong demand across hospitals, care homes, and outpatient services.

  • Generalist nursing recognised across the EU
  • Hospitals, care facilities, outpatient services
  • Specialisation options: geriatric, paediatric, intensive care
  • Clear pathway to permanent residency
Requirements: Nursing diploma/degree · German B1 or B2 (varies by state) · Medical fitness certificate · Clean criminal record
High Demand

🧒 ECDE / Early Years Teacher (Erzieher)

Germany urgently needs qualified early childhood educators. Kenyan ECDE diploma holders with B2 German can work as state-recognised early years teachers.

  • Work in Kindergartens (Kitas) across Germany
  • State-recognised professional title
  • Strong demand, especially in western states
  • Recognition possible with adaptation course if needed
Requirements: ECDE diploma · German B2 level · Under 40 years · Medically fit
High Demand

🎓 Ausbildung in Germany

Ausbildung combines practical workplace training with classroom education, giving international applicants a structured pathway to build professional skills in Germany.

  • Earn while you learn through a structured vocational program
  • Combine practical workplace experience with classroom education
  • Opportunities in nursing, healthcare, hospitality, technical trades, logistics, IT, and more
  • Guidance with German language preparation, applications, CVs, and interviews
Requirements: Recognised school or professional qualification · German language level depending on the Ausbildung (commonly B1–B2) · Valid passport · Training-specific requirements · Commitment to the full Ausbildung program
