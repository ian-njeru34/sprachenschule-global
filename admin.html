Create a lesson for a course cohort and optionally assign an approved tutor. Students with approved access to that cohort receive access to its scheduled classes automatically.

Class timetable & tutor assignments

Classes appear from 12 hours before the scheduled start until 6 hours after the scheduled end. A class shows LIVE when its tutor and at least one student are connected. Only approved tutors appear in the tutor list. Student access follows the approved course cohort selected when their account is created.
