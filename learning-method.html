How student access works

The existing website describes a registrar-approved process. Follow the latest instructions from the school for registration and payment.

STEP 01

Register

Create a student account through the existing student registration page.

Register / sign in ↗
STEP 02

Pay course fees

Use the payment instructions supplied by the school. Confirm the amount and method before paying.

Ask about fees ↗
STEP 03

Wait for approval

Submit proof of payment as instructed. The registrar confirms payment and approves eligible access.

Contact the school ↗
The current site states that approved students receive access to their assigned level and learning materials for four months from the access start date. Confirm your individual start date with the registrar.
