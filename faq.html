Frequently asked questions

Answers to help you plan your next step.

Review the information currently published by Sprachenschule Global. Contact the school to confirm details that may change.

Which language courses are listed?

The current website lists German A1, A2, B1 and B2, plus French, English, Spanish and Swahili. Contact the school to confirm current availability for other languages.

What are the published online German course fees?

The current listed online fees are KES 10,000 for A1, KES 15,000 for A2, KES 25,000 for B1 and KES 30,000 for B2. Confirm the current total fee and payment instructions before paying.

Are physical classes available?

The current homepage says physical classes are available and lists KES 5,000 additional per level. Contact the school to confirm the venue, schedule, availability and total fee.

How do I access the online learning area?

The published process is to register for a student account, pay the course fees, submit proof of payment as instructed, and wait for the registrar to approve access.

How long does online learning access last?

The current website states that approved students receive access to their assigned level and learning materials for four months from the access start date. Confirm your individual start date with the registrar.

Can I buy learning materials without enrolling in a full course?

Yes. The current website says video, audio and handout materials can be purchased separately through the learning shop.

Does the school guarantee a job, placement or visa in Germany?

The website lists language preparation and guidance related to Ausbildung, nursing and ECDE. This does not establish a guarantee of employment, placement, admission or visa approval. Ask the school to explain the exact scope of its service and verify official requirements independently.

How do I get current information?

Email sprachenschuleglobal@gmail.com, call +254 719 535 117 or contact the school on WhatsApp at +254 118 515 919.

Still have a question?

The school can help clarify course and registration details.
