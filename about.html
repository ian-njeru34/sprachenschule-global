About Sprachenschule Global

Languages can open doors to new opportunities.

Sprachenschule Global provides language training for learners in Kenya, with online learning, physical classes and Germany-focused guidance.

Who we are

A place to build language skills.

Our current website presents Sprachenschule Global as a language-learning provider serving learners in Kenya. The course catalogue includes German A1–B2, French, English, Spanish and Swahili, alongside Germany-related language preparation and guidance.

This page deliberately avoids unverified claims about the school's founding date, accreditation, staff credentials, student numbers or examination results. Those details can be added once the school confirms them.
