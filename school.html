Account access

Sign in or create your account.

Choose Student or Tutor / staff on the sign-in form. Student registrations require a course and level; access to assigned resources begins after the registrar confirms payment.

Sign in

Choose your account type, then use your registered email and password.

Access rules

  • Registration does not automatically unlock course materials.
  • Registrar approval is required after tuition payment is confirmed.
  • Students see materials for their assigned level.
  • Access expires four months after the approved start date.
  • Shop purchases are separate from course enrollment.
Join the school

Create a student account

Register once, then submit your tuition payment evidence through the school's chosen process. The registrar will approve your learning access.

Choose the language you want to study and your target level. CEFR levels run from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficient). Availability of each level is confirmed by the school.

Registrar approval

The administrator dashboard is for authorised registrars only. It can review pending students, confirm payment, assign a level and start the four-month access period.
